Looking Back

Growth Beyond
the Expected.

My On-the-Job Training experience has been one of the most unexpected but meaningful parts of my journey as a BS in Computer Science student.

Although I expected to be assigned to a company that's more aligned to the learning requirement of my course, I was assigned to a workplace that's largely related to multimedia and information. I am still grateful that I was assigned there because I was able to apply my existing technical knowledge while developing skills outside the usual areas of my course.


It gave me the opportunity to experience a real workplace and understand the responsibilities, challenges, and expectations that come with it. I learned that my knowledge and skills as a CS student can also be applied in different fields and situations.

Working in the Information Office gave me a better understanding of how data and information are handled, organized, and presented from a professional perspective. I also had the opportunity to assist neighbor offices such as the Records Office and CAO-Admin, where I was able to apply my technical knowledge and skills to practical workplace needs.


These experiences helped me become more adaptable, confident, and prepared to handle unfamiliar task and challenges.

The Takeaway

Overall, my internship was not only an opportunity to gain work experience but also a time for personal and professional growth. The lessons, challenges, and experiences I gained helped me recognize both my strengths and the areas where I still need to improve. I am grateful for this experience because it prepared me with valuable skills and knowledge that I can carry with me as I continue my journey toward my future career.
