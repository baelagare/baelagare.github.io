Stepping Out of the Code

On-the-Job Training (OJT) provides students with the opportunity to apply the knowledge and skills they have gained in the classroom to an actual workplace[cite: 1]. My internship experience at the Information Office gave me a different perspective from what I initially expected as a Computer Science student[cite: 1].

Although I was assigned primarily to multimedia-related tasks, the experience allowed me to develop skills in handling, organizing, presenting, and communicating information while also learning how these tasks are approached in a professional and organizational setting[cite: 1].

Key Responsibilities & Projects

Multimedia & Graphic Design

Redesigned NEMSULTI graphic materials, including event invitations, virtual backgrounds, and certificates[cite: 1]. Created public information materials for the 5th NEMSU Day and Buwan ng Wika[cite: 1].

Technical Support & Web

Reviewed and evaluated the official NEMSU website, identifying areas of improvement, and attended development meetings[cite: 1]. Assisted neighboring offices, like the Records Office and CAO-Admin, with hardware setups and technical troubleshooting[cite: 1].

Video Production

Edited highlight videos for the welcoming of Commissioner Ricmar P. Aquino and the 8th NEMSULTI[cite: 1]. Filmed and edited video messages for the AACUP Accreditation Survey Visits[cite: 1].

Final Reflection

My On-the-Job Training experience has been one of the most unexpected but meaningful parts of my journey as a BS in Computer Science student[cite: 1]. Working in the Information Office gave me a better understanding of how data and information are handled, organized, and presented from a professional perspective[cite: 1].

These experiences helped me become more adaptable, confident, and prepared to handle unfamiliar tasks and challenges. The lessons, challenges, and experiences I gained helped me recognize both my strengths and the areas where I still need to improve[cite: 1].
