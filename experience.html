The Internship

Stepping Out
of the Code.

On-the-Job Training provides students with the opportunity to apply the knowledge and skills they have gained in the classroom to an actual workplace. For a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science student, it is an important experience in understanding how technical knowledge can be applied in real-world situations beyond academic requirements.


My internship experience at the Information Office gave me a different perspective from what I initially expected. Although I was assigned primarily to multimedia-related tasks, the experience allowed me to develop skills in handling, organizing, presenting, and communicating information.

I was also given opportunities to assist other offices, particularly the Records Office and CAO-Admin, where I was able to apply my technical knowledge and skills to actual workplace needs and challenges.


Overall, this internship has been a meaningful part of my journey. It allowed me to strengthen my technical foundation while developing new skills, adapting to responsibilities outside my usual field, and understanding the importance of professionalism and teamwork.
